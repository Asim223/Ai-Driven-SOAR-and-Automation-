# Submission checklist

## Included

- Completed source, 29 tests, web console, training and capstone datasets.
- Incident report with UTC/WAT timeline and qualified ATT&CK mapping.
- Updated model card and playbook documentation.
- Consolidated test/metrics report and saved unit/API evidence.
- Evidence manifest and original exercise outputs.
- Seven-minute demonstration outline.
- AI-use declaration with student-provided name, ID, typed signature and corrected reflection.

## Student actions before submission

1. Review the documents, confirm the findings and explain the code independently.
2. Confirm the ID against your student record, review the corrected reflection and confirm that your typed signature meets course requirements.
3. Perform or record the seven-minute demonstration required by the course.
4. Confirm the LMS ZIP naming convention and upload limits.
5. If any file is edited or signed, regenerate its manifest hash before final upload.

The source package is a classroom prototype. Authentication, approval expiry, richer correlation and semantic AI validation are documented limitations, not completed features. Earlier AI responses and model-card versions are retained as evidence and may differ from the final submission documents.
